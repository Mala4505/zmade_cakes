# Design System: ZMade Cakes

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Atelier Ledger"**

ZMade operates like a high-end custom studio. The admin panel is the craftsperson's ledger: meticulous, warm, every entry matters. It's not a SaaS dashboard; it's the private record book of someone who takes real pride in their work. Dense enough to scan fast on a phone between baking sessions, warm enough that the interface matches the product it manages.

The customer-facing pages are the opposite in pace: unhurried, considered, with the editorial quality of a well-printed receipt from a boutique you respect. A customer opening the confirmation link has never interacted with ZMade digitally before. That first impression carries the brand entirely. It should feel like something Zainab made by hand, not generated by software.

Both surfaces share the same color foundation but differ in weight and motion. The atelier — warm cream backgrounds, teal accents reserved for the single most important action on any screen, ink tones that read warmly without being brown. The ledger — compact hierarchy, quick-read type, zero decorative clutter.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm off-white surfaces; never pure white
- Single teal accent (`#006860`), used only at the action point of highest priority
- Geist for admin text (precise, legible at any size); Cabinet Grotesk for customer headings (characterful, editorial warmth)
- Flat surfaces at rest; a single ambient lift on elevated panels
- Transitions under 200ms, ease-out-quart; no choreography in the admin, minimal choreography on customer pages

## 2. Colors: The Atelier Palette

A restrained two-tone foundation: deep teal and warm off-white. Everything else is a neutral.

### Primary
- **Artisan Teal** (`#006860`, oklch(43% 0.10 178)): The single accent. Used for primary CTAs, active navigation states, confirmation badges, and progress indicators. Nothing else. Its rarity is its authority. On any screen, teal appears at most once as a filled element.
- **Deep Atelier** (`#004d49`, oklch(34% 0.09 178)): Hover state for teal fills. Never used as a standalone fill. Always teal-deep after teal.
- **Teal Wash** (`#e6f5f4`, oklch(95% 0.025 178)): Teal at near-zero saturation. Badge backgrounds for "confirmed" status, selected row highlights, light focus rings.

### Neutral
- **Aged Cream** (`#fcf9f5`, oklch(98% 0.007 80)): The page background. Not white. This is the warmest surface in the stack; nothing should be lighter.
- **Warm Surface** (`#faf8f5`, oklch(97% 0.005 80)): Cards, panels, raised content. One step darker than cream.
- **Elevated Surface** (`#f4f0eb`, oklch(94% 0.008 80)): Hover states for rows, selected item backgrounds.
- **Hairline** (`#e8e3dd`, oklch(88% 0.008 80)): Borders, dividers, input strokes. The default separator in the system.
- **Firm Border** (`#d4cec7`, oklch(82% 0.009 80)): Emphasized borders, focused input outlines.
- **Ink** (`#191614`, oklch(15% 0.007 80)): Headlines, primary labels, the highest-contrast text. Never pure black.
- **Secondary Ink** (`#3d3936`, oklch(30% 0.006 80)): Body text, form values.
- **Muted Ink** (`#6b6461`, oklch(47% 0.006 80)): Labels, hints, metadata, timestamps. Darkened one notch from `#7a7370` (4.43:1 on cream) to ~5.5:1, comfortably clear of the 4.5:1 AA floor for the 12-13px sizes it's used at throughout the app.

### Status
- **Grove** (`#2e7d52`): Success states, "delivered" badges.
- **Amber Bark** (`#92600f`): Warning states, "pending" priority.
- **Terracotta** (`#c13434`): Errors, danger actions, "cancelled" badges.
- **Slate Blue** (`#0e6ba8`): Informational states only.

### Named Rules
**The One Voice Rule.** Teal (`#006860`) appears as a filled color on at most one element per screen. It marks the primary action. If two elements both want to be teal, one of them is wrong. The second most important action gets a ghost or secondary treatment.

**The Warm Base Rule.** `#fff` is prohibited as a background. The floor is Aged Cream (`#fcf9f5`). Cards sit on Warm Surface (`#faf8f5`). Never go lighter than the floor.

## 3. Typography

**Admin Font:** Geist (sans-serif, from Google Fonts)
**Customer Heading Font:** Cabinet Grotesk (from Fontshare, loaded via next/font/local) — fallback: Bricolage Grotesque
**Mono Font:** Geist Mono (from Google Fonts, for KWD amounts, order IDs, token display)

**Character:** Geist reads with technical authority at small sizes — precise without being cold, the right tool for a dense phone interface. Cabinet Grotesk carries editorial warmth in customer headings: its slightly rounded geometry references high-end print collateral without the showiness of a pure display face.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Cabinet Grotesk, 800, clamp(2rem → 3.5rem), lh 1.05, ls -0.02em): Customer page headlines only. The cake name, the occasion. Never used in admin.
- **Headline** (Cabinet Grotesk, 700, clamp(1.375rem → 2rem), lh 1.2, ls -0.015em): Customer page section headers. Admin uses Title weight instead.
- **Title** (Geist, 600, 1rem, lh 1.35, ls -0.01em): Admin section labels, card headings, form group titles. The primary hierarchy step in the admin panel.
- **Body** (Geist, 400, 15px, lh 1.6): Prose, form values, detail text. Max 65ch line length.
- **Label** (Geist, 500, 13px, lh 1.4, ls +0.01em): Form labels, table column headers, nav items, metadata.
- **Mono** (Geist Mono, 400, 14px, lh 1.5): KWD prices (KD 12.500), order IDs, tracking tokens, confirmation tokens.

### Named Rules
**The Mono Numbers Rule.** Every KWD amount, order ID, date-time stamp, and UUID token is set in Geist Mono. Numbers in proportional type look wrong next to each other. Mono keeps them aligned and immediately scannable — critical when Zainab is checking prices at a glance.

**The Admin Headline Rule.** Cabinet Grotesk is forbidden in the admin panel. It's the customer-facing face of ZMade; its appearance in the tool would blur the register boundary. Admin headings use Geist 600.

## 4. Elevation

This system is flat by default. Surfaces are distinguished by background tint, not shadows. The tonal stack (cream → warm surface → elevated surface) communicates depth without a shadow in sight — which reads better on warm-tinted backgrounds than grey box-shadows.

One exception: modals and floating popovers receive a single structural shadow to separate them from the page without the context disappearing behind them.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Floating** (`0 8px 32px rgba(25, 22, 20, 0.12), 0 2px 8px rgba(25, 22, 20, 0.06)`): Modals, command palettes, date picker popovers. The only shadow used in the system.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-By-Default Rule.** Cards, panels, sidebars, nav drawers: no box-shadow. Tonal background difference is the only depth signal. If you reach for a box-shadow on a card, reconsider the structure — the shadow is masking a layout problem, not solving it.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
A button's weight is its only communication — the hierarchy is encoded in fill, not size.

- **Shape:** Gently curved corners (8px radius)
- **Primary:** Teal fill (`#006860`), cream text (`#fcf9f5`), 12px/24px padding (vertical/horizontal). Font: Geist 500, 14px. No border.
- **Hover/Focus:** Background deepens to `#004d49` in 150ms ease-out-quart. Focus ring: 2px offset, teal-light (`#e6f5f4`) fill, firm border (`#d4cec7`) outer stroke.
- **Secondary:** Elevated surface fill (`#f4f0eb`), secondary ink text (`#3d3936`). Same shape and padding. Hover: hairline border appears.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, secondary ink text. Hover: elevated surface tint appears.
- **Danger:** Terracotta fill (`#c13434`), cream text. For cancel/delete actions. Used sparingly.
- **Disabled:** Hairline background, muted ink text, no hover state, `cursor: not-allowed`.

### Status Badges / Chips
Small, pill-shaped, read-only status communicators. Used throughout the inquiry queue and order board.

- **Shape:** Full radius (9999px), 3px top/bottom padding, 10px left/right padding, 13px Geist 500
- **Pending:** Elevated surface bg, muted ink text
- **Awaiting Confirmation:** Amber Bark text (`#92600f`) on amber wash background
- **Confirmed:** Teal deep text (`#004d49`) on teal wash bg (`#e6f5f4`)
- **In Progress:** Slate blue text on blue wash
- **Ready:** Grove text on green wash
- **Delivered:** Muted ink on hairline bg
- **Cancelled:** Terracotta text on terracotta wash

### Cards / List Items
The admin panel's primary content unit for inquiry and order rows.

- **Corner Style:** 8px radius
- **Background:** Warm Surface (`#faf8f5`) on Aged Cream page
- **Shadow Strategy:** None. Tonal difference communicates the lift.
- **Border:** Hairline (`#e8e3dd`), 1px
- **Internal Padding:** 16px (md spacing)
- **Hover:** Background shifts to Elevated Surface (`#f4f0eb`) in 120ms ease-out. No scale transform.
- **Selected/Active:** Left indicator suppressed (see Don'ts). Teal accent border top, 2px, full width.

### Inputs / Fields
Restrained, functional. The admin fills these often; they should have no friction.

- **Style:** Cream background (`#fcf9f5`), hairline border (`#e8e3dd`), 8px radius, 10px/14px padding
- **Focus:** Border color shifts to Firm Border (`#d4cec7`). Subtle teal-light glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px #e6f5f4`).
- **Label:** 13px Geist 500, ink-muted (`#6b6461`), 6px gap above input.
- **Error:** Border shifts to terracotta (`#c13434`). 12px Geist 400 error text below in terracotta.
- **Disabled:** Elevated surface bg, muted ink text, no border change. `cursor: not-allowed`.
- **Select/Dropdown:** Same base style. Chevron icon in muted ink. Options rendered as native or headless (not styled-components dropdown).

### Navigation (Admin)
Mobile-first bottom navigation for the admin; collapses to a sidebar on wide screens.

- **Mobile bottom bar:** Aged Cream background, hairline top border. 5 icon+label items. Active item: teal icon + teal label (13px, 500 weight). Inactive: muted ink icon + label.
- **Desktop sidebar:** 240px wide. Warm Surface background. Group labels in 11px Geist 500 uppercase, muted ink, 0.06em tracking. Active item: teal-light bg, teal text, no side stripe.
- **No side-stripe borders on active items.** Active state is communicated by teal text + teal-light background fill, not a left border stripe.

### Order Kanban Columns
Signature component for the orders board.

- **Column header:** 14px Geist 600, ink-secondary. Count badge in muted ink.
- **Column background:** Elevated Surface (`#f4f0eb`).
- **Card within column:** Warm Surface bg, hairline border, 8px radius. Customer name in title weight; cake details in 13px label; event date in mono. Priority indicator: top-border stripe (2px) — normal: none; high: amber bark; urgent: terracotta.
- **Drag state:** Floating shadow on dragged card. Column highlight on drop target: teal-light tint.

### Customer Confirmation Page (Signature)
The brand-forward surface. Uses Cabinet Grotesk for headings.

- **Page background:** Aged Cream. Full bleed, no outer container chrome.
- **ZMade wordmark/header:** Cabinet Grotesk 800, ink, centered.
- **Section heading:** Cabinet Grotesk 700, 1.375rem, ink.
- **Order detail rows:** Two-column label/value grid. Label: 13px Geist 500, muted ink. Value: 15px Geist 400, ink-secondary.
- **Editable fields:** Standard input style above, but 16px minimum font size (prevents iOS zoom).
- **Confirm button:** Full-width on mobile, primary teal. The One Voice Rule means this is the only teal element on the page.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use Aged Cream (`#fcf9f5`) as the page background — never pure `#ffffff` or `#fff`.
- **Do** reserve teal (`#006860`) for the single highest-priority action per screen (confirm order, primary CTA, active nav item).
- **Do** render all KWD prices and order IDs in Geist Mono for visual alignment.
- **Do** use Cabinet Grotesk only on customer-facing pages (`/confirm/*`, `/track/*`). Keep it off the admin panel.
- **Do** keep transitions under 200ms and always use ease-out-quart (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 1, 0.5, 1)`, matching `lib/motion.ts`'s `EASE_OUT_QUART` and `app/globals.css`'s `--ease-out-quart`). Ease-in curves are prohibited.
- **Do** use touch targets of at minimum 44px tall on all interactive elements (mobile-first admin).
- **Do** communicate active navigation state with background fill + text color only — no side-stripe border.
- **Do** use tonal background shifts (cream → warm surface → elevated surface) for depth. Shadows are for floating elements only.
- **Do** respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: wrap all Framer Motion animations with `useReducedMotion` checks.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use pure `#000000` or `#ffffff` anywhere. Every neutral must carry the warm tint.
- **Don't** use the teal accent as a decorative element, section separator, or heading color. Its only roles are: primary button fill, active state indicator, confirmed badge.
- **Don't** add a colored left-border stripe to cards, list items, or nav items as an active/alert indicator. This is explicitly banned. Use background tint or top-border instead.
- **Don't** apply gradient text (`background-clip: text`). Use solid ink colors with weight variation for emphasis.
- **Don't** use Inter, Roboto, or any neutral system sans on customer pages. Cabinet Grotesk (or Bricolage Grotesque as fallback) is the customer-facing heading face.
- **Don't** make this look like a food-delivery app (Talabat/Careem): no promotional badge clusters, no price-highlight chips, no "Order Now" energy. ZMade is already booked.
- **Don't** make this look like a generic SaaS dashboard (Notion/Linear clones): no cold grey-on-white surfaces, no flat icon+heading+text card grids, no enterprise-dashboard density without warmth.
- **Don't** make the customer pages look like a Pinterest-core bakery site: no cursive/script fonts, no pastel photo overlays, no decorative flour/ribbon motifs in the UI chrome.
- **Don't** produce flat corporate form pages for customer-facing routes: the confirmation and tracking pages are brand moments, not government portals.
- **Don't** animate layout CSS properties (`width`, `height`, `padding`, `top`, `left`). Animate only `transform` and `opacity`.
- **Don't** add box-shadows to cards, sidebars, or nav panels. The flat-by-default rule is absolute for resting states.
- **Don't** scale elements from 0 for entry animations. Minimum scale for entering elements: 0.96. Pair with opacity 0→1.
